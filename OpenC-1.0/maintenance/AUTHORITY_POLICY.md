
# Authority policy

There is one current development authority. Historical archives, proposals, accepted-but-unintegrated changes, implementations, and tests cannot override it.

A conflict is resolved by:

1. opening a finding;
2. identifying affected grammar, rules, diagnostics, implementation, and fixtures;
3. classifying the compatibility impact;
4. approving a correction or proposal;
5. integrating every affected artifact together;
6. recording the change.

Silent implementation divergence is forbidden.
