# Contributing to OpenC

Contributions are welcome when the project has a publication channel capable
of receiving them.

By submitting a contribution, you certify that you created it or otherwise
have the right to submit it, and that it may be distributed permanently under
the repository policy:

- software and standalone code under 0BSD;
- specifications, documentation, metadata, diagrams, artwork, and other
  non-code assets under CC0-1.0.

No contributor license agreement is required. Contributions must identify any
third-party material and its license. Do not submit confidential information,
trade secrets, credentials, or material that cannot be redistributed under the
applicable project terms.

Normative language changes must include the coordinated standard, grammar,
metadata, diagnostic, conformance, rationale, and compatibility updates
required by `GOVERNANCE.md`.
