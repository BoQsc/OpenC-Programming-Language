
# License and governance status

The legal and governance decision remains pending.

Before public release, an authorized project owner or governing body must select and record:

- normative-standard license;
- compiler, runtime, library, and tooling license;
- conformance-corpus license;
- contribution terms;
- name and trademark policy, if any;
- normative change authority;
- security-reporting channel;
- release-signing authority;
- support and errata policy.

This development tree does not make those legal choices implicitly.
