# OpenC license policy

This repository uses two permissive legal instruments. File-level SPDX
identifiers override the directory defaults below.

## Software — 0BSD

The compiler, bootstrap compiler, runtime, standard-library implementation,
tools, build and release programs, tests, maintained programs, source examples,
and executable conformance fixture sources are licensed under the Zero-Clause
BSD license (`SPDX-License-Identifier: 0BSD`). The complete terms are in
`LICENSE`.

## Specifications and creative material — CC0-1.0

Normative and informative specification text, documentation, schemas, grammar,
rule metadata, conformance metadata, diagrams, artwork, and other non-code
assets are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal
(`SPDX-License-Identifier: CC0-1.0`). The complete terms are in
`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`.

Code fragments embedded in CC0 documentation may be reused under CC0. The same
or equivalent fragments appearing as standalone source files remain under
0BSD.

## Boundaries

These terms cover only material whose copyright is owned or controlled by the
OpenC Project contributors. Third-party material, if introduced, must be
identified with its own notice and compatible terms. CC0 does not waive patent,
trademark, privacy, publicity, or third-party rights. Project-name policy is
recorded separately in `TRADEMARKS.md`.
