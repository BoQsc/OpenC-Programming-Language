# Errata policy

The project owner is the errata authority. Confirmed defects are classified as:

- normative: the specification is contradictory, ambiguous, or incorrect;
- implementation: the reference implementation differs from the standard;
- conformance: a fixture or expected result is incorrect or incomplete;
- editorial: meaning is unchanged.

Normative, implementation, and conformance corrections receive a stable erratum
identifier, affected-version range, severity, resolution, regression evidence,
and compatibility note. Editorial corrections may be committed directly when
they cannot change meaning. Published 1.0 artifacts remain immutable; corrected
artifacts use a new version.
